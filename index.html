About me

I build human-aware AI.

I’m Kumar Akash, a Principal Scientist at Honda Research Institute USA. I study how intelligent systems can understand people—their goals, cognitive states, trust, and behavior—and use that understanding to collaborate more effectively.

My work connects human cognition, machine learning, and autonomous systems to create AI that is adaptive, transparent, and responsive to the people around it.

Current project

Human-aware AI for better teamwork.

My current work asks how intelligent agents can become useful teammates—aware of human needs, social context, team structure, and shared goals.

Current2026

Human-AI Teaming

Understanding how proactive AI roles, real-time signaling, and team composition shape collaborative problem solving. The goal is human-aware AI that knows when to facilitate, when to contribute as a peer, and how to support a team without disrupting it.
