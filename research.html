01 · Current

Human-AI Teaming

What makes an AI agent a good teammate—not just a capable tool?

This is my current human-aware AI project. It examines how proactive agents influence group performance, workload, trust, exploration, and communication, comparing facilitator, peer, and specialist roles in time-sensitive and ad hoc collaboration.

02 · Earlier project

Prosocial intelligence & well-being

Can autonomous systems help people become more cooperative without overriding their agency?

Across vehicles, delivery robots, and micromobility, this work studies reciprocity, helping behavior, intent, and well-being. It combines behavioral experiments with computational models that reason about latent prosocial state.

03 · Earlier project

Adaptive mobility & shared control

How should automation adapt when people differ in skill, preference, attention, and readiness?

This research predicts takeovers, learns preferred driving styles, and explores shared control across cars and personal mobility devices. The aim is personalization that supports both safety and subjective well-being.

Trust dynamics & feedback control

Beginning with real-time trust sensing and dynamic state models, this project developed feedback-control frameworks that vary automation transparency to keep trust calibrated to system capability.

Research trajectory

A decade of increasingly human-aware autonomy.

The questions have expanded—from control of physical systems, to estimation of human state, to reciprocal intelligence operating in hybrid human-machine societies.

2020

2022

2024

2026
